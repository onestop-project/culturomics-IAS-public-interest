## Repeatable workflow culturomics

Copyright (C) 2026 Theresa Henke, Ana Novoa https://onestop-project.eu/

A repeatable workflow has been developed as part of the Horizon Europe project OneSTOP (One Biosecurity Systems and Technology for People, Places, and Pathways) to address the human dimensions of biological invasions. Specifically, the developed workflow aims to i) assess public interest in Invasive Alien Species (IAS), and ii) analyze the presence and dynamics of IAS in e-commerce as a proxy for potential introduction pathways.

The workflow is showcased on the basis of two case studies. In case study 1, we analysed public interest in the 88 species listed on the list of Union concern between 2016 and 2022 in all 27 current EU member states (MS) as well as the UK. Using Google health, we compiled weekly search-query values for each species-country combination. We conducted a peak analysis of the data and identified a total of 153 peaks that were included for further assessment. For each of these peaks we performed a manual search on Google search enginge in the adjacent time period, aiming to identify the reasons why users searched for each particular species. Using pre defined categories, we documented information on the specific angles and topics of the content covered, the platforms it was shared on, and the stakeholders associated.

In case study 2, we assessed the presence of IAS listed on or proposed for the Union List in e-commerce. Online retailers, peer-to-peer trading platforms, and two common e-commerce platforms, were surveyed in 6 EU member states (Czech Republic, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain) in 2021 and 2024.

Both the workflow and associated R codes are available in this repository.

This repository is still a work in progress.
